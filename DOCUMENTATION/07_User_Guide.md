# 07. User Guide

## 1. Citizen Portal Workflow
1. **Register / Login:** Navigate to `/register`, create a Citizen account, and log in.
2. **Submit Grievance:** Click **File a Public Grievance**, enter the subject, district, and detailed description, attach supporting proof (optional), and click **Submit Grievance**.
3. **Save Reference ID:** Note the generated Reference ID (e.g., `CMO-2026-48291`).
4. **Track Progress:** Enter your Reference ID on the **Track Grievance** page to view the step-by-step audit timeline.

## 2. CMO Administrator Workflow
1. **Monitor KPIs:** Log in with a `cmo_admin` account to view Total, Pending, Resolved, and Overdue grievance counts.
2. **Filter & Review:** Filter grievances by district, status, or AI-assigned priority, and read the 2-sentence AI CMO Summary.
3. **Assign to Department:** Click **Forward**, select the target Department and Department Officer, add instructions, and submit.

## 3. Department Officer Workflow
1. **View Assigned Cases:** Log in with a `department_officer` account to see grievances forwarded to you.
2. **Update & Resolve:** Open a grievance, change its status (`In Progress` or `Resolved`), enter resolution remarks, upload an **Action-Taken Report** PDF/image, and save.
